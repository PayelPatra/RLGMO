
# Data

The RLGMO framework is designed to work with clinical datasets containing relevant patient and medical information, including entities such as diseases, symptoms, medications, patient characteristics, and other clinically relevant attributes.

The dataset used in the research experiments is not included in this repository. However, the provided preprocessing and modeling pipeline can be adapted to other clinical datasets with compatible entities and attributes.

Users can provide their own appropriately structured clinical dataset and modify the preprocessing configuration as required for the specific dataset.
